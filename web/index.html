Keyboard & changing disks

Click the screen to send your keyboard to the Spectrum. Arrow keys use the Spectrum cursor combinations: Caps Shift + 5/6/7/8. Shift is Caps Shift; Alt is Symbol Shift. Search and other controls keep their normal keyboard behavior.

Use the on-screen keys on a touch device, or to confirm a disk prompt:

When a program asks for the next disk, select it and choose Insert without reset, then press the key requested on screen. Play disk starts a fresh boot. Archived test images may need manual commands or may not boot by themselves.

The browser emulator is a listening and viewing preview. Project timing and release qualifications remain those recorded in the repository.
